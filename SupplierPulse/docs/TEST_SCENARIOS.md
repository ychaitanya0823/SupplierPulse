## Unit tests (local, Node)
- `scripts/test_impact.js`: business-impact maths (2,400 units, USD 192,000, expedite USD 9,400, net USD 182,600) and the "impact reported by site" case.
- `scripts/test_validator.js`: 15 cases — real output → approval; disallowed action, unapproved alternate, cap over policy, positive disruption claims, missing expedite clause → manual review; low confidence → warning only; negated phrases pass (incl. "doesn't require", "needs", curly apostrophes).
- `scripts/test_other_supplier.js`: 6 cases for the loop's policy check (valid proposal, empty agent output, cap over policy, unapproved alternate, disruption claim, missing contract text).
- `scripts/test.js`: exposure maths for the three suppliers (distances, radius, days of cover, risk score).
